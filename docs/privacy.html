Server Two

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated October 2026

Overview

Server Two is a pickleball scorekeeper for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. The developer receives none of your data and runs no server that stores it.

Data the App Stores

Your matches, scores, saved players (names and optional photos you choose) and match history are stored on your device. The iPhone app syncs this history across your own devices using iCloud (the CloudKit private database of your Apple Account). That data belongs to you, is only readable through your iCloud account, and is not available to the developer.

Your app settings stay on the device they were set on. The Apple Watch app keeps matches on the watch and sends them to your iPhone using Apple's device-to-device connection. Your saved player names (never photos) also go from the iPhone to the Apple Watch over the same connection, so you can pick players on the watch, and names you type on the watch go back to the iPhone with the match.

Apple Health and Heart Rate

On Apple Watch, the app can record each match as a pickleball workout and show your live heart rate while you score. This happens only if you allow it in the Health permission prompt, and you can turn it off in the app or in Health settings. The workout is saved to Apple Health on your devices. Heart rate is displayed during the match and is not stored in the app's match history. This health data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Photos

If you pick a photo for a player, the app receives only the photo you choose, shrinks it, and stores it with that player. It is not sent anywhere except to your own iCloud along with the rest of your history.

Live Activities and Voice

Live Activities show the current score on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. They are created and updated on your device. The optional Voice Announcer uses your device's built-in speech voices.

Analytics & Tracking

Server Two uses no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

In-App Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or transmit any payment information. Purchases are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting Your Data

Delete matches and players inside the app, or delete the app to remove its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Server Two.

Children's Privacy

Server Two does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts and no sign-up.
