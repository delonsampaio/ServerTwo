Server Two: Pickleball Tracker

Privacy Policy. Last updated: 2026-10-31

Server Two is a pickleball scorekeeper for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not collect your data. This page explains where your information lives.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple frameworks.
  • The developer receives none of your data and runs no server that stores it.

What the app stores

Your matches, scores, saved players (names and optional photos you choose) and match history are stored on your device. The iPhone app syncs this history across your own devices using iCloud (the CloudKit private database of your Apple Account). That data belongs to you and is only readable through your iCloud account. It is not available to the developer. Your app settings stay on the device they were set on.

The Apple Watch app keeps matches on the watch and sends them to your iPhone using Apple's device-to-device connection.

Apple Health and heart rate

On Apple Watch, the app can record each match as a pickleball workout and show your live heart rate while you score. This happens only if you allow it in the Health permission prompt, and you can turn it off in the app or in Health settings. The workout is saved to Apple Health on your devices. Heart rate is displayed during the match and is not stored in the app's match history. This health data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not see your payment details.

Live Activities and voice

Live Activities show the current score on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. They are created and updated on your device. The optional Voice Announcer uses your device's built-in speech voices.

Photos

If you pick a photo for a player, the app receives only the photo you choose, shrinks it, and stores it with that player. It is not sent anywhere except to your own iCloud along with the rest of your history.

Deleting your data

Delete matches and players inside the app, or delete the app to remove its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, use Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Server Two.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
