Common questions

How much does it cost?
You can play one full match for free. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited matches. There is no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

My match history is not showing on my other device.
History syncs through your private iCloud. Check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for Server Two (Settings, your name, iCloud), and that the device is online. Syncing can take a short while.

The Watch app does not show up.
Install Server Two on your iPhone, then open the Watch app on the iPhone and install Server Two on your watch. The watch app also works on its own, and finished matches reach your iPhone when the two are connected.

The heart rate or workout is not recorded.
Allow Health access when the watch asks. You can change it in the Health app, under Sharing, Apps, Server Two, and in the Record workout switch on the watch.

The score does not appear on my Lock Screen.
Make sure Live Activities are allowed for Server Two in iPhone Settings.
