Getting Started

How do I score a match?

Start a new match from the Play tab, choose singles or doubles, enter the players, and flip the coin to see who serves first. Then tap a team's card to score a point. Press and hold a card to correct a disputed score.

Who serves first, and in the next game?

The coin flip (or your own choice) decides the first server of game one. In later games the first server alternates between the two teams, as in the official rulebook.

How do I know which player stands where?

In doubles side-out games the scoring screen shows which partner is on the left or right court. Press and hold a score card to swap them if you started the other way round.

When do we change ends?

Between games the teams always switch sides, and the Game Over screen says so. In the deciding game the app also tells you when a team reaches the mid-game switch score.

I picked the wrong player. Can I fix it afterwards?

Yes. Open the match in History and tap Edit Players to swap a player or fill in a blank team. You can also delete a match from History.

Sound, Haptics and Lock Screen

What does the Voice Announcer do?

It reads the score out loud after each point, using your iPhone's built-in speech voices. Turn it on or off in Settings under Audio & Haptics. Haptics have a switch there too.

The score does not appear on my Lock Screen.

Server Two shows the live score as a Live Activity during a match. Check that Live Activities are allowed for Server Two in the iPhone's Settings, and that the Live Activity switch in the app's Settings is on.

Apple Watch

How do I get the Watch app?

Install Server Two on your iPhone, then open the Watch app on the iPhone, find Server Two under Available Apps and tap Install. The Watch app also works on its own, with no iPhone nearby.

Does a match I start on the iPhone appear on the Watch?

No. The Watch app keeps its own score. A match you finish on the Watch is sent to your iPhone's History after you tap Done.

The heart rate or workout is not recorded.

The Watch asks for Health access the first time you start a match. You can change it in the Health app under Sharing, Apps, Server Two. The Record workout switch in the Watch app's Rules turns the workout off or on.

Pro and Purchases

How much does Server Two cost?

You can play one full match for free. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited matches on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no subscription, and Pro works for your whole family with Family Sharing.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?

Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Your Data

My match history is not showing on my other device.

History syncs through your private iCloud. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for Server Two (Settings, your name, iCloud), and that the device is online. Syncing can take a short while.

How do I delete my data?

Delete matches and players inside the app, or delete the app to remove its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Server Two.
